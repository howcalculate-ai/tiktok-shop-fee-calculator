# TikTok Shop Fee Calculator

Selling on TikTok Shop involves more than choosing a product price. Sellers need to account for platform fees, product costs, shipping, creator commissions, advertising expenses, and other costs before they can understand their actual profit.

This repository provides a practical introduction to calculating TikTok Shop selling costs and estimating profit.

## What You Can Calculate

- TikTok Shop referral fees
- Product costs and shipping expenses
- Creator or affiliate commissions
- Estimated profit per order
- Profit margins
- Selling prices based on a target profit
